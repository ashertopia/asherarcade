FAQ
Common questions
How many questions can I include?

20 is the standard game. It takes about 5 minutes to play, which is long enough to be fun and short enough that people finish. Ask if you want more or fewer.

How does scoring work?

Every question has a 20-second clock. A right answer is worth 1,000 points if you answer instantly, sliding down to 500 at the buzzer. A wrong answer is worth 0. Getting it right matters most, and speed decides who finishes first and who finishes second.

Can a guest play more than once?

No. A phone that has finished the game can't start again, and each name can only be used once. If a phone dies mid-game, it picks up where it left off. You can remove a name or let someone replay from your scoreboard sheet.

Can you build trivia around photos?

Yes. Send us the photos and the questions ("who is this?", "match the year", "which groomsman said this?") and we'll wire them in. Works great for weddings and family reunions.

How does the leaderboard work?

Every guest types their name and plays on their own phone, whenever they want and from wherever they are. When they finish they see their score and their place on the leaderboard, and it keeps updating live as more family members finish.

Can guests play on their phones?

Yes. The game is browser-based and works on iOS, Android, desktop, and older phones. No app to install — guests tap the link and they're in.

How long does it take to build?

Most games are ready within 7 days of your order. Have an event this weekend? Put the date on your order form and we will tell you right away if we can make it.

How long does the game stay live?

The game keeps working as long as we host it. The first 3 months of hosting are free, and your leaderboard is saved the whole time. After that it's $19 a year to keep it live. If you don't renew, we quietly retire the link.

Do you offer discounts?

Yes. Discounts are available for active military, veterans, first responders, teachers, and homeschool families. Tell us about your group when you reach out and we will let you know what we can do.
