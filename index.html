FAQ

Common questions

Do guests need to download an app to play?

No. Every game is browser-based and works on any phone, tablet, or computer. Guests tap the link and start playing. There is nothing to install and no account to create.

How long does it take to build a custom game?

Most games are ready within 7 days. Rush orders with a 3-day turnaround are available. Add the date you need it by on the order form and we will tell you whether the timeline works.

Can I see the game before I pay?

You can play any of our live games at asherarcade.com to get a feel for the quality. You pay through Stripe when you order, and we share a preview before final delivery so you can ask for fixes.

What occasions are custom games good for?

Weddings, baby showers, gender reveals, birthdays, graduations, anniversaries, church events, youth groups, and company parties. If there is a person or a moment worth celebrating, we can build something around it.

How does the leaderboard work?

Every game gets its own leaderboard, separate from every other game we build. Only the people you share your link with can play, so the scores on it are your friends and family, nobody else. Guests enter a display name and compete for the top spot, it updates in real time on any device, and there is nothing for you to set up. Scores stay saved for the 3 months of free hosting and as long as you renew after that.

What is KeepsakeDrop?

KeepsakeDrop is a guest photo collection app. Guests scan a QR code at your event and upload photos straight from their phone, with no app to install. The photos land in a private Google Drive folder we create for your event and share with you from day one, so you can watch them arrive. The collection window runs through 30 days after your event, and then the folder is handed over to you.

Do you offer discounts?

Yes. Discounts are available for active military, veterans, first responders, teachers, and homeschool families. Tell us what the game is for when you reach out and we will let you know what we can do.
