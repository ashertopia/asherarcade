FAQ

Common questions

Do guests need to download an app to play?

No. Every game is browser-based and works on any phone, tablet, or computer. Guests tap the link and start playing. There is nothing to install and no account to create.

How long does it take to build a custom game?

Most games are ready within 7 days. Rush orders with a 3-day turnaround are available. Mention your date when you reach out and we will tell you whether the timeline works.

Can I see the game before I pay?

You can play any of our live games at asherarcade.com to get a feel for the quality. For a commissioned game we share a preview before final delivery, and you do not pay the balance until you are happy with it.

What occasions are custom games good for?

Weddings, baby showers, gender reveals, birthdays, graduations, anniversaries, church events, youth groups, and company parties. If there is a person or a moment worth celebrating, we can build something around it.

How does the leaderboard work?

Every custom game includes a live leaderboard. Guests enter a display name when they play and compete for the top score. It updates in real time and works on every device, with no setup on your end.

What is KeepsakeDrop?

KeepsakeDrop is a guest photo collection app. Guests scan a QR code at your event and upload photos straight from their phone, with no app to install. The photos land in a private Google Drive folder we create for your event and share with you from day one, so you can watch them arrive. The collection window runs through 30 days after your event, and then the folder is handed over to you.

Do you offer discounts for churches or nonprofits?

Yes. We offer 50% off the first game for Christian churches, ministries, homeschool families, active military, veterans, and first responders. It runs on the honor system with no paperwork required. Mention it when you reach out.
