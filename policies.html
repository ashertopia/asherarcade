Our Policies

Plain-English terms, refunds, and privacy for everything Asher Arcade makes.

Who we are

Asher Arcade (asherarcade.com) is operated by Scott Asher LLC. All orders, payments, and services on this site — and Asher Arcade listings on marketplaces like Etsy — are provided by Scott Asher LLC. Questions? Email hello@asherarcade.com or use the contact form — we reply within 24 hours.

Orders & payment

  • Games are sold at our published prices ($39–$129 per game; original games from $249, quoted before work starts). Nothing is owed until you place an order or approve a quote.
  • Games are paid in full through Stripe when you order. Extras are billed with a separate Stripe link once we confirm them. Original games are quoted, and payment is due when you approve the quote.

Refunds & our guarantee

  • If your product doesn't work at your event and we can't make it right, you get a full refund. That's the whole policy. Tell us within 7 days of your event.
  • Custom builds include a revision round before your event. We test every build on real phones before delivery.
  • Digital and personalized products (including Etsy orders) aren't returnable once delivered, but the guarantee above still applies — if it doesn't work, we fix it or refund it.
  • Etsy orders are additionally covered by Etsy's purchase protection.

What we will and won't create

  • Our free games are for everyone. We serve every customer, and anyone can play or share them.
  • Custom games, reveals, and announcements are original creative work that we design and write. Asher Arcade is a Christian-owned business, and we only create custom content we can stand behind.
  • We don't create custom work that includes nudity or sexual content, profanity or vulgar language, or anything obscene.
  • We don't create custom wedding games or announcements that celebrate a marriage other than one between one man and one woman.
  • We don't build reveals, invitations, QR codes, or links that send people to events or pages with any of the content above.
  • This is about the message, not the person. We decline the same content no matter who asks, and we never turn anyone away for who they are.
  • We review every custom order before we start building. If we can't take a request, we'll tell you right away and refund what you paid in full.

What you get & what you can do with it

  • Your custom game or tool is yours to share and play at your event and beyond. Hosting on asherarcade.com is free for the first 3 months, with your leaderboard saved. After that, hosting renews at $19 per year.
  • We keep the right to show anonymized versions of games in our portfolio unless you ask us not to.
  • Our free tools (Quiz Builder, game demos) are provided as-is for personal use.

Privacy

  • KeepsakeDrop photos collect in a private Google Drive folder we create for your event and share with you before your event — you can watch photos arrive live. During the collection window (through 30 days after your event) that folder lives in our Google Drive so we can verify uploads are working; we access it only for support and never share it with anyone else. After the window closes we hand the album over to you (you download it, or ask us to transfer the folder into your own Google account). We keep our copy, and our access, for 60 days after the window closes so you have time to claim it; after that we permanently delete our copy and our access ends — we send reminders before that happens. KeepsakeDrop questions: keepsakedrop@asherarcade.com
  • Quiz Builder quizzes live entirely in the share link. There is no account and no server database of your quiz content.
  • The contact form collects your name, email, and message only so we can reply. We never sell or share your information.
  • This site uses Google Analytics to count visits. Leaderboards store only the display name a player chooses to enter.
  • To have any information removed, use the contact form.

The boring-but-necessary part

Products are provided as-is to the fullest extent allowed by law; Scott Asher LLC's total liability for any order is limited to the amount you paid for it. These policies may be updated over time — the version on this page is the one in effect.
