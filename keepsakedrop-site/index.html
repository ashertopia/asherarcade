Questions

Good to know

Do my guests need to install anything?

No. They open their phone’s camera, point it at the QR code, and tap the link. The upload page works in any browser — no app, no account, no sign-in.

Where do the photos go?

Into a private Google Drive folder we create for your event and share with you before the big day — you watch photos arrive in real time. While collection is open we keep access so we can make sure everything is working; when it closes, the folder is handed over to you entirely and we delete our access. You then have a full 60 days to download your album or take ownership — no paywall, ever — after which we permanently delete our copy.

How long can guests upload?

From the moment you receive your link through 30 days after your event date — plenty of time for everyone to get home from the honeymoon and share what they captured.

What do I receive after booking?

Within 24 hours: your private guest link, and a print-ready, letter-size QR sign designed with your names — ready for table cards or a welcome-table frame.

What about videos?

KeepsakeDrop collects photos. For videos, we suggest guests send them to you directly — video files are too large for reliable on-the-spot uploads on venue Wi-Fi.

Can I try it first?

Yes — preview the guest experience right now. It’s a demo, so nothing is actually uploaded.
