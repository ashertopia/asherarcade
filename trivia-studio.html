2. Look and rules

School or party colors. The game fine-tunes the shades so every word stays readable, whatever you pick.

3. Questions

20 is the sweet spot (about 5 minutes to play). Tap the circle next to the right answer. Keep answers short so they fit on the buttons. A question photo is optional and most questions don't need one. The story shows after each answer, and it's where the laughs are.

Paste questions from a doc

One question per block, with a blank line between blocks. Put the question on the first line and one answer on each line after it. Mark the right answer with *. A line starting with > is the story.

What did Maya want to be when she was 6?
Veterinarian
*Astronaut
Pop star
> She wore a cardboard space helmet all of first grade.
